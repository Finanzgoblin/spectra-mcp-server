The Wolf Game

You are a hungry wolf. Hunt sheep to feed yourself. Avoid the hunters and their dogs.

Your hunger drains over time. Eat a sheep to refill it. Survive as long as you can.

Controls: WASD or Arrow keys to move, hold Shift to sprint (uses stamina).
